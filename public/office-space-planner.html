Office Space Planner FAQ

Common questions before you start viewing offices

These answers explain what the planner can estimate and where an actual floor plan still needs professional review.

How much office space do I need per employee in Singapore?
A single square-foot-per-person figure can be misleading. Two companies with the same headcount may need very different office sizes depending on workstation dimensions, meeting rooms, private offices, pantry, storage and circulation. This planner therefore calculates from your actual requirements rather than using one fixed allowance per employee.
Does this planner include meeting rooms and private offices?
Yes. The recommendation includes the meeting rooms, director rooms, manager rooms, phone or Zoom rooms and support spaces that you select. If you do not select a space, the planner does not automatically add it.
Can a small SME use an office below 1,000 sqft?
Yes. Small teams can operate from offices well below 1,000 sqft when their room requirements are limited and the floor plate is efficient. The planner is designed to handle smaller SMEs as well as larger offices, so it does not impose a 1,000 sqft minimum.
Why does circulation affect the recommended office size?
Furniture and room footprints are not the whole office. People still need workable access between desks, rooms and shared facilities. The planner therefore allows for overall planning and circulation instead of simply adding the physical size of each desk and room.
Does this result confirm SCDF compliance?
No. This is a space-planning and property-search estimate, not an SCDF compliance assessment. Final suitability still depends on the actual unit, exit routes, floor-plan geometry and the final proposed office layout. Fire-safety requirements should be reviewed during detailed design where applicable.
